HKGCC Business-School Partnership Programme

With bridging the gap between Hong Kong’s business community and secondary schools as our mission, the HKGCC Business-School Partnership Programme aims to offer students invaluable insights into the corporate world, and equipping them with the knowledge and skills needed to navigate their careers.

Programme Details

The programme provides valuable opportunities and key growth benefits for all participating students, schools, and corporate partners;

workspace_premium

For Schools: Student Empowerment

Students can gain real-world industry exposure, practical career insights, and guidance to help bridge classroom knowledge with professional practice.

corporate_fare

For Companies: Engagement with Future Talent

Companies can connect directly with students from a variety of backgrounds and skills, engage with the educational sector, and contribute to youth development in Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area.

There are three main objectives for the Business-School Partnership Programme;

hub

Bridge Schools & Companies

Bridge the gap between educational institutions and the professional corporate world.

groups

Executive Connections

Connect students with active and experienced business executives.

cognition_2

Career Exploration

Deepen students' understanding of businesses and diverse career fields.

Launched by the Chamber over two decades ago, the HKGCC Business-School Partnership Programme cooperates in conjunction with a variety of connecting organizations and intiatives;

school

AEMSS

Since 2001, the Chamber has cooperated with the Association of English Medium Secondary Schools to deliver this programme.

local_library

HKCMSA

The Chamber has also worked with The Association of Hong Kong Chinese Middle Schools since 2013.

arrows_output

Greater Bay Area Expansion

Since the programme's 24th edition, the programme will include the Greater Bay Area. This will exchange between local businesses and secondary schools in the Mainland, enriching the learning experience and broadening students’ horizons.

The programme is held within the academic year of 2026/7.

Programme Procedure

Rundown

The rundown of the Business-School Partnership Programme can be divided into three main stages;

1
Invitation and
Application
  • Invitations are sent to schools under different associations and member companies.
  • Interested schools and voluntary companies may send in the application before October.
2
Briefing and
Matching
Meeting
  • Each voluntary company with be matched with one school to develop various activities for the entire school year.
  • A briefing and matching meeting allows both parties to get a better understanding of their roles and objectives and exchange contacts.
3
Activity
Arrangements
Both parties can exchange views on future manpower requirements and student expectations through activities such as company visits, career talks, interview workshops, job shadowing, etc.
